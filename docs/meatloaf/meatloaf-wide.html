Meatloaf
Serves 4
Prep ~25 min
Cook ~60 min
Prep

Set up the loaf

Ground beefOnionBreadcrumbsEggMilkDijonHoneyButter
  1. Heat oven to 350 F. Measure the milk before mixing so the loaf stays tender but not loose.
  2. Finely dice onion and soften it in butter with a pinch of salt for 5-6 minutes. Cool briefly.
  3. Mix beef, breadcrumbs, egg, milk, softened onion, Dijon, salt, and pepper gently. Stop as soon as it holds together.
Glaze

Honey mustard top

HoneyDijonYellow mustardButter
  1. Whisk honey, Dijon, yellow mustard, and melted butter until glossy.
  2. Shape meatloaf on a lined pan or in a loaf pan. Brush with half the glaze.
  3. Keep the rest of the glaze for the halfway brush.
Cook

Bake and rest

  1. Bake until the loaf is beginning to set, about 35 minutes.
  2. Brush on the remaining glaze, then keep baking until the center reaches 160 F.
  3. Rest 10 minutes before slicing so the juices stay put.
  4. Serve with horseradish potatoes and extra mustard if you want it sharper.
