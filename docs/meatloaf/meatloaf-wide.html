Meatloaf
Serves 4
Prep ~25 min
Cook ~60 min
Prep

Set up the loaf

Ground beefOnionBreadcrumbsEggMilkDijonWorcestershire
  1. Heat oven to 350 F. Measure the milk before mixing so the loaf stays tender but not loose.
  2. Finely dice 1 small onion and soften it in butter with a pinch of salt for 6-8 minutes. Cool briefly.
  3. Mix beef, breadcrumbs, egg, milk, softened onion, Dijon, Worcestershire, salt, and pepper gently. Stop as soon as it holds together.
Glaze

Horseradish ketchup top

KetchupDijonHorseradishWorcestershire
  1. Whisk 1/3 cup ketchup, 1 tbsp Dijon, 1 tbsp brown sugar or honey, 1 tsp Worcestershire, 1-2 tsp horseradish, and black pepper.
  2. Shape meatloaf on a lined pan. Brush with half the glaze.
  3. Keep the rest of the glaze for the halfway brush.
Potatoes

Green onion mash

PotatoesButterMilkGreen onions
  1. Cut 1.5 lb potatoes, cover with cold salted water, and boil when the meatloaf hits the 40-minute glaze check.
  2. Boil 15-18 minutes, drain, then mash with 3 tbsp butter and 1/4 cup warm milk.
  3. Fold in 3 sliced green onions. Season with salt and pepper.
Green Beans

Garlic butter finish

Green beansButterGarlic
  1. Trim 12 oz green beans. Cook in 1 tbsp butter over medium-high for 5-7 minutes.
  2. Add 1 minced garlic clove and cook 30-60 seconds.
  3. Finish with black pepper and a tiny squeeze of lemon if you have it.
Cook

Bake and rest

  1. Bake until the loaf is beginning to set, about 40 minutes.
  2. Blot grease, brush on the remaining glaze, then keep baking until the center reaches 160 F.
  3. Rest 10 minutes before slicing so the juices stay put.
  4. Serve with green onion mashed potatoes and garlic butter green beans.
