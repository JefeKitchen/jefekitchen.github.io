Bowl Night
Carnitas Elote Bowls
Crispy carnitas, lime rice, creamy street-corn topping, avocado, and bright crunchy finishes.
— • —
Base
Cilantro Lime Rice
Fluffy · bright · sturdy
Rice tossed with lime, cilantro, and a little butter or oil so the bowl starts fresh instead of plain.
Main
Crispy Carnitas
Savory · browned edges · juicy middle
Leftover carnitas crisped hard in a skillet, then hit with lime so it feels newly cooked.
Top
Elote Corn
Creamy · smoky · tangy
Corn folded with mayo, sour cream, cotija, lime, chili powder, and green onion.
Finish
Avocado, Cabbage, and Hot Sauce
Cool · crunchy · loud
Avocado, shredded cabbage, extra lime, cilantro, and hot sauce keep the rich carnitas from feeling heavy.
The usual rice bowl, but with street-corn swagger.
