Honey Garlic
Serves 2-3
Prep ~10 min
Cook ~30 min
Prep

Chicken and sauce

ChickenHoneyGarlicSoy sauceRiceCucumber
  1. Start rice first if it is not already cooked.
  2. Mix honey, soy sauce, garlic, and a splash of water for the pan sauce.
  3. Pat chicken dry and season lightly. The sauce brings salt too.
Green Beans

Keep them snappy

Green beansOilSaltPepper
  1. Trim green beans. Heat a skillet with oil over medium-high.
  2. Cook beans until bright green and blistered in spots, about 5-7 minutes.
  3. Salt lightly and move them aside before they go soft.
Skillet

Glaze the chicken

  1. Sear chicken until browned and mostly cooked through.
  2. Lower heat, add sauce, and simmer until glossy. Spoon sauce over chicken as it thickens.
  3. Cook chicken to 165 F. If sauce tightens too much, loosen with a splash of water.
  4. Serve over rice with green beans and cucumber on the side.
