Grilled Chicken Sandwiches
Serves 2–3 · ~45 minutes
Prep
Before the fire

Chicken and sauce setup
  1. Slice chicken breasts into thin cutlets or pound them even. Thin, even chicken is your friend on charcoal.
  2. Season with olive oil, kosher salt, pepper, garlic powder, Dijon, and lemon juice. Let sit while the grill heats.
  3. Mix mayo with a spoonful of horseradish, lemon juice, black pepper, and a tiny splash of chicken broth. Keep the rest of the broth for jus.
Charcoal Setup
Same idea as gas, different controls

Direct and cooler side
  1. Open both vents. Light a 2/3 full chimney and wait until the coals are ashed over around the edges.
  2. Dump coals on one side only. Put the grate on, close the lid for 5 minutes, then scrape and oil.
  3. For sandwiches, you want a lively medium-hot fire, not a raging steakhouse fire. If your hand can stay over the hot side for only 1-2 seconds, cook mostly on the cooler edge of the coal pile.
Grill
Fast cook

Chicken, cheese, buns
  1. Cook chicken over direct heat for 3-5 minutes per side. Move to the cooler side if it browns before the center is done.
  2. Cook to 165 F. Add cheese during the last minute, close the lid, and let it melt.
  3. Toast buns over the cooler side or very briefly over direct heat. They go from perfect to sad quickly, so watch them.
Jus
Broth moment

Horseradish broth jus
  1. Simmer 3/4 cup chicken broth with 1 tsp Dijon until slightly concentrated.
  2. Off heat, stir in 1 tbsp horseradish, lemon juice, and black pepper.
  3. Spoon a little over the chicken before building. Mix another spoonful into mayo for the bun sauce.
Sides
Keep it sandwich-friendly

Smoky slaw and chips
  1. Toss cabbage slaw with mayo, lemon, Dijon, a little horseradish, salt, and pepper.
  2. If you want more grill practice, char corn next to the chicken and fold the kernels into the slaw.
  3. Serve sandwiches with slaw and chips or pickles.
