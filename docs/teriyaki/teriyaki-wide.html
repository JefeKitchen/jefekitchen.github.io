Pineapple Orange Teriyaki
Serves 2-3
~45 minutes
Rice

Start the base

RiceCoconut milkWaterSalt
  1. Start rice first so it can steam while everything else comes together.
  2. Use coconut milk for part of the liquid if you want it richer.
  3. Keep covered off heat once done.
Sauce

Pineapple orange glaze

PineappleOrangeSoy sauceGarlicGinger
  1. Mix pineapple juice, orange, soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and sweetener if needed.
  2. Simmer until it tastes bright and slightly thickened.
  3. Keep a little sauce aside for drizzling at the end.
Cook

Finish the spread

  1. Cook chicken until browned and nearly done.
  2. Add teriyaki sauce and simmer until glossy and the chicken reaches 165 F.
  3. Make the cucumber side while sauce reduces: cucumber, acid, salt, and a little sweetness.
  4. Serve chicken over coconut rice with cucumber on the side.
