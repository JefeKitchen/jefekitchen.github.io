Prep First
Do First · All Dishes

Break Down the Pineapple

🍍
  1. Cut the top and bottom off the pineapple. Stand it upright and slice off the skin in strips from top to bottom. Remove any remaining eyes.
  2. Cut into quarters lengthwise and remove the core from each quarter.
  3. From one quarter, cut small bite-sized chunks for the cucumber bites. Set aside.
  4. From another quarter, cut slightly larger chunks for the finished teriyaki dish. Set aside.
  5. Squeeze or press the remaining pineapple over a bowl to collect pineapple juice for the sauce — you need about 3 tbsp.
Make Ahead · Starter

Cucumber Bites

🥒
  1. Slice the cucumber into rounds about ¼ inch thick.
  2. Top each round with a small piece of fresh pineapple.
  3. Drizzle lightly with honey and add a small pinch of salt over the whole tray.
  4. Add a small mint leaf on top of each if using. Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve. These can be made an hour ahead.

Coconut Rice
Start Before Chicken

Coconut Rice

🍚
  1. Rinse 1.5 cups jasmine rice under cold water until the water runs mostly clear.
  2. Combine in a pot with 1 can coconut milk plus enough water to bring the total liquid to 2.25 cups. Add ½ tsp salt and 1 tsp sugar.
  3. Bring to a boil over medium-high, then reduce to low, cover, and cook 18 minutes. Do not lift the lid.
  4. Remove from heat and rest covered 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork. It will hold just fine while you finish the chicken.
Teriyaki Chicken
Mix the Sauce

Teriyaki Sauce

🥢
  1. In a small bowl whisk together ¼ cup soy sauce, 3 tbsp honey, 3 tbsp pineapple juice, juice of 1 orange, 3 garlic cloves minced, and 1 tsp fresh grated ginger.
  2. In a separate small bowl mix 1 tbsp cornstarch with 2 tbsp cold water until smooth. Set aside — this thickens the sauce at the end.
One Skillet

Sear & Sauce the Chicken

🍳
  1. Pat chicken thighs dry. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Sear chicken smooth-side down 5–6 minutes without moving until golden. Flip and cook 4 minutes more. Remove to a plate — not fully cooked yet, that's fine.
  3. Drop heat to medium. Pour the teriyaki sauce into the same pan and bring to a simmer, scraping up any browned bits.
  4. Stir in the cornstarch slurry. The sauce will thicken within a minute — stir constantly. If it gets too thick add a small splash of water.
  5. Return the chicken to the pan. Add the pineapple chunks. Spoon sauce over everything. Simmer on low 5–6 minutes until chicken is cooked through and sauce is glossy.
  6. Garnish with sesame seeds and sliced green onion if using.

Serve
Plating

Putting It Together

🍽️
  1. Put the cucumber bites out first while you plate the main — they're the starter and keep people busy.
  2. Spoon coconut rice into bowls or onto plates. Lay the chicken on top and spoon the teriyaki sauce and pineapple chunks generously over everything.
  3. Finish with sesame seeds and green onion if using. Serve immediately.

On the Sauce

The cornstarch slurry thickens fast — have everything ready before you add it and keep stirring. If the sauce gets too thick before the chicken goes back in, add a small splash of water and stir.

⏱️

Total Time · ~45 Minutes

Make cucumber bites first — they sit in the fridge. Start rice before the chicken. Rice takes 23 minutes total, chicken about 20. Everything lands at the same time.
