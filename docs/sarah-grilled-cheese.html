A Note on HeatThe stove will be hot. This is intentional and not a malfunction. Do not be alarmed.

Prep
Sarah · Mise en Place

Gather the Ingredients

🧀
  1. Locate 2 slices of bread. They should be next to each other in the bag. This is called a pair.
  2. Source 2 slices of cheese. American melts well and requires no further explanation or justification.
  3. Remove 1 tbsp of butter from the fridge. Set on the counter. It will soften. This is fine.
Sarah · Technical Step

The Butter Application

🧈
  1. Spread softened butter evenly across one side of each slice of bread. This is the outside of the sandwich. Take a moment to identify which side is the outside. Both sides are correct until they aren't.
  2. The butter coverage should be edge to edge. Unbuttered corners are the leading cause of an uneven crust and a disappointing life.
Bella · Showing Up

Butter Inspection

🐾
  1. Bella has arrived. She cannot have butter. She knows this. She is here anyway. Give her a look and move on.
Cook
Sarah · Heat Management

Prepare the Pan

🍳
  1. Place a pan on the stove over medium-low heat. Not high. Medium-low. This distinction is load-bearing.
  2. Wait 1–2 minutes for the pan to come to temperature. This is called patience. It is a virtue. It will save the cheese.
Sarah · Assembly

Build & Cook the Sandwich

🥪
  1. Place one slice of bread butter-side down in the pan. The sizzle is normal. The sizzle is good.
  2. Lay both slices of cheese on top. Coverage matters. Gaps will haunt you.
  3. Place the second slice of bread on top, butter-side up. The sandwich is now a sandwich. Take a moment.
  4. Cook for 3–4 minutes until the bottom is deep golden brown. Do not press it down with a spatula. Do not touch it. Do not look at it too long. Leave it alone.
Sarah · The Critical Moment

The Flip

🔄
  1. Slide a spatula fully under the sandwich. Commit to this. A hesitant flip is a failed flip.
  2. Flip in one confident motion. It is on the other side now. This was the hard part. It is over.
  3. Cook another 2–3 minutes until the second side is equally golden and the cheese is fully melted. Press gently with the spatula. Just once. You've earned it.
Finish
Sarah · Plating

The Presentation

🍽️
  1. Remove from heat. Place on a cutting board.
  2. Cut diagonally. Not straight across. Diagonally. This is non-negotiable and the difference between a sandwich and an experience.
  3. Plate and serve immediately. Accept all compliments with grace. Deflect no credit.

Total Time

About 10 minutes. If it takes longer, that is okay. If the cheese is not melted, it needed more time. If the bread is black, the heat was too high. We said medium-low.
