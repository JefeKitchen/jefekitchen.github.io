Charcoal grill · Serves 2-4

Charcoal Chicken

Prep
  1. 1
    Fill a charcoal chimney with lump charcoal or briquettes and light it. Let it burn until the top coals are covered in a light gray ash (about 15-20 minutes) so the heat is clean and stable.
  2. 2
    While the grill heats, pat chicken thighs dry. Season with 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt, pepper, 1 tsp smoked paprika, grated garlic, lemon zest, and a light coat of olive oil. Let sit at room temperature.
Grill
  1. 1
    Pour the hot coals entirely onto one side of the grill, leaving the other side completely empty. This creates a hot "searing" zone and a cooler "convection oven" zone. Oil the grate.
  2. 2
    Place chicken skin-side down directly over the hot coals. Sear for 3-4 minutes per side until beautifully charred. (If dripping fat causes major fire flare-ups, temporarily slide the chicken to the cool side).
  3. 3
    Move the charred chicken over to the cooler, empty side of the grill. Cover with the lid, positioning the open lid vents directly over the chicken to draw the hot air across the meat.
  4. 4
    Cook for 15-20 minutes until an instant-read thermometer hits 175 F in the thickest part. Rest 5-10 minutes to lock in the juices before slicing.
Sauce
  1. 1
    Simmer 1 cup chicken broth with 1 tbsp Dijon, 1 tsp honey, and a squeeze of lemon until slightly reduced.
  2. 2
    Take off heat. Whisk in 2 tbsp sour cream and 1-2 tbsp horseradish. Keep warm, not boiling.
  3. 3
    Spoon sauce over sliced chicken and finish with parsley.
