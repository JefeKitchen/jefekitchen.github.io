Charcoal grill · Serves 2-4

Charcoal Chicken

Mix
  1. 1
    Pat chicken thighs dry. Season with 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt, pepper, 1 tsp smoked paprika, grated garlic, lemon zest, and a light coat of olive oil.
  2. 2
    Let the chicken sit while the grill heats. Even 20 minutes helps.
Grill
  1. 1
    Set the charcoal grill for two zones: coals on one side, cooler side empty. Oil the grate.
  2. 2
    Sear chicken over the coals for 3-4 minutes per side, then move to the cooler side.
  3. 3
    Cover and cook until the thickest piece hits 165 F. Rest 5-10 minutes.
Sauce
  1. 1
    Simmer 1 cup chicken broth with 1 tbsp Dijon, 1 tsp honey, and a squeeze of lemon until slightly reduced.
  2. 2
    Take off heat. Whisk in 2 tbsp sour cream and 1-2 tbsp horseradish. Keep warm, not boiling.
  3. 3
    Spoon sauce over sliced chicken and finish with parsley.
