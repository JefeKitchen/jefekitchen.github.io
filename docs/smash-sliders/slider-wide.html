Smash Sliders
Serves 2-3
~45 minutes
Prep

Build the station

Ground beefSlider bunsCheeseSaucesPicklesOnion
  1. Divide beef into loose balls. Do not pack them tight.
  2. Slice buns and stage cheese, toppings, sauces, and plates before heat starts.
  3. Heat the griddle or skillet until very hot.
Cook

Smash and cheese

Beef ballsSaltCheeseBuns
  1. Place beef balls down and smash firmly into thin patties.
  2. Season immediately. Cook until edges are browned and lacy.
  3. Flip, add cheese, and cook just long enough to finish.
Assemble

Keep it moving

  1. Toast buns on the griddle while patties finish.
  2. Build sliders in the planned order so each one gets sauce, patty, cheese, and toppings.
  3. Serve immediately. Smash sliders are best hot, messy, and not overthought.
