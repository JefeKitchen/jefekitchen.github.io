French Press · Stovetop

Caramelized Onions

25–30 min
  1. Slice 2 onions paper thin.
  2. Melt a generous knob of butter in a pan on medium-low.
  3. Add onions and a pinch of salt. Stir occasionally. Do not rush or crank the heat. They need the full time to get deep golden and jammy.
  4. Once done, pull off heat and set aside. They'll stay warm on their own.
While Onions Cook
2
All Sliders · Cold Prep

Onions, Sauce & Cheese

10 min
Onions
  1. Slice 1 onion paper thin for smashing into The Bad Boy patties. Set aside in a small pile.
  2. Slice 1 more onion paper thin for the raw topping on The Motor City Closer. Keep in a separate pile.
Spicy Sauce
  1. Mix 3 tbsp mayo + 1.5 tbsp sriracha + 1 tsp yellow mustard + splash of pickle juice. Stir and set aside.
Cheese
  1. Slice or shred Pepper Jack, Gruyère, and Sharp Cheddar into three separate piles. Set near the flat top.
Right Before Cooking
3
All Sliders · Final Prep

Portion the Beef & Heat Up

5 min
  1. Loosely portion beef into 1.5 oz balls — roughly 12 total. Do not compress. Set on a tray.
  2. Crank flat top to high and let it fully preheat. A drop of water should instantly vaporize.
  3. While flat top heats, butter the cut sides of the Hawaiian rolls and have them ready to toast.
Cook
4
Flat Top · All Sliders

Toast the Buns

  1. Drop flat top to medium-high. Place rolls cut-side down. Toast 1–2 min until golden. Work in batches. Pull and set aside.
5
Slider 01 · Flat Top

The Bad Boy

  1. Crank flat top back to high. Place 4 meat balls down with space between them.
  2. Place a small pile of shaved onion on top of each ball immediately.
  3. Lay parchment over each ball. Smash hard within 10 seconds. Press and hold 3–4 seconds. Get them thin.
  4. Season with salt and pepper. Cook 90 sec – 2 min without touching. Edges brown; top goes gray halfway up.
  5. Scrape hard and fast, getting fully under the crust. Flip.
  6. Lay Pepper Jack on each patty. Splash water nearby, dome 30–45 sec to melt.
  7. Build: spicy sauce on both buns · patty · pickled jalapeños on top.
6
Slider 02 · Flat Top

The French Press

  1. Same smash process as The Bad Boy — no onion on top before smashing.
  2. Flip, add Gruyère, dome to melt 30–45 sec.
  3. Build: thin smear of Dijon on bottom bun · thin smear of horseradish · patty · big pile of caramelized onions on top.
7
Slider 03 · Flat Top

The Motor City Closer

  1. Same smash process — no onion on top before smashing.
  2. Flip, add Sharp Cheddar, dome to melt 30–45 sec.
  3. Build: yellow mustard on bottom bun · patty · raw shaved onion · dill pickle chips on top.
🔥

Run All Three Back to Back

Each batch takes about 3 minutes. Run Bad Boy first, French Press second, Closer last. Keep the flat top ripping hot between batches and don't let it sit cold.
