Instructions
Slider Prep

Caramelized Onions (02) — Start First

Melt butter in a pan on medium-low. Add 2 thinly sliced onions and a pinch of salt. Stir occasionally for 25–30 min until deep golden and jammy. Don't rush or crank the heat.

Onion Prep (01 & 03)

Slice one onion paper thin. Keep a pile for smashing into 01 and a separate pile for the raw topping on 03.

Spicy Sauce (01)

3 tbsp mayo + 1.5 tbsp sriracha + 1 tsp yellow mustard + splash of pickle juice. Stir and set aside.

Beef Balls

Loosely portion into 1.5 oz balls — roughly 12 total. Do not compress. Set on a tray.

Cheese

Slice or shred all three cheeses into separate piles next to the flat top.

Flat Top · All Sliders

Toast the Buns

🍞
  1. Set flat top to medium-high. Butter the cut sides of the rolls generously.
  2. Place cut-side down. Toast until golden, 1–2 min. Work in batches. Pull and set aside.
Slider 01 · Flat Top

Spicy Onion Smash

🌶️
  1. Crank flat top to high. Place 4 meat balls down with space between them.
  2. Place a small pile of shaved onion on top of each ball immediately.
  3. Lay parchment over each ball. Smash hard within 10 seconds. Press and hold 3–4 seconds. Get them thin.
  4. Season with salt and pepper. Cook 90 sec – 2 min without touching. Edges brown; top goes gray halfway up.
  5. Scrape hard and fast, getting fully under the crust. Flip.
  6. Lay Pepper Jack on each patty. Splash water nearby, dome 30–45 seconds to melt.
  7. Build: spicy sauce on both buns · patty · pickled jalapeños on top.
Slider 02 · Flat Top

French Onion

🧅
  1. Same smash process as 01 — no onion on top before smashing.
  2. Flip, add Gruyère, dome to melt 30–45 seconds.
  3. Build: dijon on bottom bun · thin smear of horseradish · patty · big pile of caramelized onions on top.
Slider 03 · Flat Top

Classic Sharp

🥒
  1. Same smash process as 01 — no onion on top before smashing.
  2. Flip, add Sharp Cheddar, dome to melt 30–45 seconds.
  3. Build: yellow mustard on bottom bun · patty · raw shaved onion · dill pickle chips on top.
🔥

Timing the Hit

Start caramelized onions first — they need 25–30 min. Slaw goes in the fridge early. Tots in the oven next. Tots come out right as you finish the last slider batch. Load queso on tots at the last second and hit the table all at once.

Cook hot · eat fast · no leftovers
