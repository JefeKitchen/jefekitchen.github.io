Overnight Oats
5 grab-and-go breakfasts · Greek yogurt base
Prep
Batch for the week

Set up 5 jars
  1. Put out 5 jars or containers with lids.
  2. Into each jar, add 1/2 cup rolled oats, 1/2 cup Greek yogurt, and 1/2 cup milk.
  3. Add 1 tsp chia seeds, 1 tsp honey or maple, a small splash of vanilla, and a pinch of salt to each jar.
  4. Stir each jar well, scraping the bottom so there are no dry oat pockets.
Flavor
Keep it easy

Make a few versions
  1. Berry vanilla: add berries and a little extra vanilla.
  2. Banana peanut butter: add sliced banana and a spoonful of peanut butter.
  3. Apple cinnamon: add diced apple, cinnamon, and a small drizzle of honey.
  4. If using granola or nuts, pack them separately so they stay crunchy.
Fridge
No morning work

Chill and grab
  1. Lid the jars and refrigerate at least 6 hours, ideally overnight.
  2. In the morning, grab a jar and go. Eat cold, straight from the container.
  3. If a jar gets too thick, stir in a splash of milk before eating. For true grab-and-go, add that splash the night before.
  4. Best texture is days 1-4. Day 5 is still fine, just softer.
