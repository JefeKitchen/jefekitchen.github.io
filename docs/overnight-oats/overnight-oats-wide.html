Overnight Oats
Serves 5
Prep ~10 min
Chill overnight
Base

Fill the jars

JarsRolled oatsGreek yogurtMilkChiaHoneyVanilla
  1. Put out 5 jars with lids.
  2. Add oats, Greek yogurt, milk, chia seeds, honey or maple, vanilla, and a pinch of salt to each jar.
  3. Stir well, scraping the bottom so there are no dry oat pockets.
Flavors

Make a few versions

BerriesBananaPeanut butterAppleCinnamonGranola
  1. Berry vanilla: add berries and a little extra vanilla.
  2. Banana peanut butter: add sliced banana and peanut butter.
  3. Apple cinnamon: add diced apple, cinnamon, and honey.
Fridge

Chill and grab

  1. Lid jars and refrigerate at least 6 hours, ideally overnight.
  2. Pack granola or nuts separately if you want crunch.
  3. Grab and eat cold. Best texture is days 1-4; day 5 is softer but fine.
