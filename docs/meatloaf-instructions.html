Jaya · Glaze

Make the Glaze

🍯
  1. Whisk together 3 tbsp honey, 2 tbsp Dijon, 1 tbsp yellow mustard, and 1 tbsp melted butter.
  2. Set aside. You'll use half before the oven and half halfway through.
Theo · Again

Honey Taste Test

🐾
  1. Theo is already here and he has been waiting. Let him have a tiny lick. He hasn't learned anything but neither have you — you're making meatloaf again.
Jeff · Knife Work

Soften the Onion

🔪
  1. Finely dice ½ white onion.
  2. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a small pan over medium-low. Add the onion and a pinch of salt. Cook 5–6 minutes until soft and translucent. Don't brown them.
  3. Pull off heat and let cool for a few minutes before adding to the meat mixture.
Jeff · The Main Event

Mix the Meatloaf

🥩
  1. In a large bowl combine 1.5 lbs ground beef, 1 large egg, ½ cup breadcrumbs, and ¼ cup milk — measured, in a cup, not from the carton.
  2. Before adding anything else — pinch off a 2-inch chunk of plain beef, mix in a tiny splash of milk, and roll it into a small ball. Set it aside on the corner of the sheet pan. This is Theo's Meatball. It goes in the oven with everything else, plain and safe.
  3. Add 2 tbsp yellow mustard, 1 tbsp Dijon, 1 tbsp prepared horseradish, 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce, 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp black pepper, ½ tsp garlic powder, and the cooled softened onion.
  4. Mix until just combined. Do not overmix — work it too hard and it gets dense and tough. Stop as soon as everything is incorporated.
  5. Transfer to a parchment-lined sheet pan and shape into a loaf roughly 9 inches long and 4 inches wide. Place Theo's Meatball on the corner of the same pan.
  6. Brush the top only with a thin layer of glaze. Not the sides — the beef will release fat as it bakes and wash it off anyway. The heavy glaze on the sides comes at the 40-minute mark.
Cook
Jeff · Oven · 350°F

Bake the Meatloaf

🔥
  1. Put the meatloaf in the oven. Set a timer for 40 minutes.
  2. At 40 minutes, blot any large grease pools around the meatloaf with a paper towel. Then brush the remaining glaze generously over the top and sides — the loaf has finished releasing most of its fat so the glaze will stick and caramelize now.
  3. Pull Theo's Meatball off the pan at this point — it's done. Set it aside to cool before giving it to him.
  4. Continue baking another 15–20 minutes until the internal temp hits 160°F.
  5. Pull from the oven and rest 10 minutes before slicing. Don't skip the rest — it holds together much better.
Jaya · Start When Meatloaf Goes In

Horseradish Mashed Potatoes

🥔
  1. Peel and cut 1.5 lbs potatoes into rough chunks. Add to a pot and cover with cold salted water. No need to turn the burner on yet — just get them ready.
  2. When Jeff does the 40-minute glaze check, turn the burner on high and bring to a boil. This gives Jaya a relaxed runway — no rushing.
  3. Boil 15–18 minutes until a fork slides in easily. Drain well.
  4. Return to the pot. Add 3 tbsp butter, ¼ cup warm milk, 1–2 tbsp prepared horseradish, salt and pepper. Mash until smooth.
  5. Taste and adjust — more horseradish for more bite, more butter for richness. Hold on lowest heat until the meatloaf is rested and ready to slice.
Theo · His Moment

The Theo Meatball

🐾
