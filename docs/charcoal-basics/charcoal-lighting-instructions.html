Lighting Charcoal
Chimney starter · lighter cube · two-zone fire
Before Lighting
Setup

Open the grill and stage the chimney
  1. Open the bottom vent and lid vent all the way. Charcoal gets hotter with more airflow.
  2. Set the chimney starter on the charcoal grate, not on a table or deck surface.
  3. Put 1 lighter cube under the chimney. Fill the chimney about 2/3 full for a normal two-zone cook.
Light
Do not use lighter fluid

Let the chimney work
  1. Light the cube through one of the lower chimney holes.
  2. Leave the chimney alone for 15-20 minutes. The bottom coals light first and the fire climbs upward.
  3. The coals are ready when the top pieces are gray around the edges and you can see orange glow down in the chimney.
  4. If the top is still mostly black, wait. Dumping too early makes the grill frustrating and uneven.
Build the Fire
Two-zone default

Hot side and safe side
  1. Carefully pour the lit coals onto one side of the grill. Leave the other side mostly empty.
  2. Put the cooking grate on, close the lid, and preheat for 5 minutes.
  3. Scrape the grate clean. Oil it lightly with paper towel held in tongs if food may stick.
  4. Put the lid vent over the cooler side so heat and smoke travel across the food instead of straight out.
Control Heat
Air is the gas knob

Adjust vents slowly
  1. More open vents mean hotter. More closed vents mean cooler.
  2. Make small changes and give the grill a few minutes to respond.
  3. For flare-ups, move food to the cool side and close the lid. Do not panic-flip everything over the fire.
  4. When finished, close both vents to starve the coals of oxygen. Empty ash only when fully cool.
