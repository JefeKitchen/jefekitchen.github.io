Charcoal grill · 4 sandwiches

Grilled Chicken Sandwiches

Prep
  1. 1
    Slice chicken breasts into thin cutlets or pound to even thickness. Pat dry.
  2. 2
    Season with olive oil, kosher salt, pepper, garlic powder, Dijon, and a squeeze of lemon.
  3. 3
    Toast buns, slice toppings, and set cheese nearby before the chicken hits the grill.
Grill
  1. 1
    Set the charcoal grill for two zones. Cook chicken over direct heat for 3-5 minutes per side.
  2. 2
    Move to the cooler side if it browns too quickly. Cook to 165 F.
  3. 3
    Add cheese during the last minute, cover, and let it melt. Rest chicken for 5 minutes.
Horseradish Broth Jus
  1. 1
    Simmer 3/4 cup chicken broth with 1 tsp Dijon until slightly concentrated.
  2. 2
    Off heat, stir in 1 tbsp horseradish, lemon juice, and black pepper.
  3. 3
    Use a spoonful on the chicken, then mix another spoonful into mayo for the bun sauce.
Build
  1. 1
    Spread horseradish mayo on both buns.
  2. 2
    Stack chicken, lettuce, tomato, and pickles. Add a little jus right before closing.
