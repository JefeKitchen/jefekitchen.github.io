Tonight's Recipe
HONEY
GARLIC
CHICKEN
Serves 2–3
Time ~25 Min
Dishes 1 Skillet
Ingredients
Cucumber½
Chicken thighs4–6
Garlic cloves6
Green beans2 handfuls
Honey3 tbsp
Soy sauce3 tbsp
Chicken broth½ cup
Cornstarch1 tbsp
Butter1 tbsp
Olive oil1 tbsp
Make Ahead — Cucumber Bites
  1. 1
    Slice cucumber into rounds about ¼ inch thick. Arrange on a plate.
  2. 2
    Drizzle with honey and a small splash of soy sauce. Sprinkle sesame seeds if you have them. Cover and refrigerate until ready.
🐾
Theo — Act I · Hopeful
Theo has smelled the honey and is now present. Give him a plain cucumber round — he's allowed. He will accept this graciously and immediately want another one.
Prep
  1. 1
    Mince 6 garlic cloves. This is a lot of garlic. That's correct. That's the dish.
  2. 2
    Trim ends off the green beans. Set aside.
  3. 3
    Whisk together 3 tbsp honey, 3 tbsp soy sauce, and ½ cup chicken broth in a small bowl. Set aside.
  4. 4
    Mix 1 tbsp cornstarch with 2 tbsp cold water in a separate small bowl until smooth. Set aside.
  5. 5
    Pat chicken thighs dry. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
· · · ·
Cook — Start the Rice First
  1. 1
    Rinse rice under cold water until mostly clear. Combine with water per package instructions in a pot. Bring to a boil, reduce to low, cover, and cook 18 minutes. Do not lift the lid.
  2. 2
    Once done, remove from heat and rest covered 5 minutes. It'll hold just fine while you finish the chicken.
· · · ·
Cook — Sear the Chicken
  1. 1
    Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high. Sear chicken smooth-side down 5–6 minutes without touching until golden. Flip and cook another 4 minutes. Remove to a plate.
😤
Theo — Act II · Impatient
The cucumber was not enough. He is aware something better is happening on the stove. He is pacing. He is not subtle about it.
Cook — Build the Sauce
  1. 1
    Drop heat to medium. Add butter. Add minced garlic and cook 1–2 minutes, stirring constantly, until fragrant and just golden. Do not let it burn.
  2. 2
    Pour in the honey soy broth mixture. Bring to a simmer, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom.
  3. 3
    Stir in the cornstarch slurry. Sauce thickens quickly — keep stirring. Add a splash of water if it gets too thick.
  4. 4
    Return chicken to the pan with the green beans. Spoon sauce over everything. Simmer on low 5–6 minutes until chicken is cooked through and beans are tender-crisp.
· · · ·
Plate & Serve
  1. 1
    Serve over rice. Spoon extra sauce generously over everything — it's the best part.
  2. 2
    Garnish with fresh parsley if using.
🐕
Theo — Act III · The Payoff
Give him a small piece of plain cooked chicken — no sauce. He has earned it. He will be very pleased with himself. All is forgiven.
⏱️

Total Time · ~25 Min

Start rice before you sear the chicken and everything lands together. The garlic step is the only one that needs your full attention — don't walk away from it.
