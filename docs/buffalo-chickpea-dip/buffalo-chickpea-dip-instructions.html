Buffalo Chickpea Dip
Serves 4 · ~25 minutes
Pull Out
Ingredients
Chickpeas Plain hummus Hot sauce Lemon Green onions Fresh garlic Smoked paprika Cayenne Olive oil Pita Small baking dish Chips
Make Dip
Mild base, optional heat

Build the base
  1. Heat oven to 400 F. Drain and rinse 1 can chickpeas.
  2. Mash about two-thirds of the chickpeas in a bowl. Leave the rest mostly whole so the dip has some texture.
  3. Stir in 1/2 cup plain hummus, 2 tbsp buffalo-style hot sauce, 2 tsp olive oil, 1 small grated garlic clove, and 1/4 tsp smoked paprika.
  4. For a medium batch: add another 2 tbsp buffalo-style hot sauce and 1/8 tsp cayenne. Skip this step for a mild batch.
Bake
Warm and scoopable

Finish it
  1. Spread into a small baking dish. Drizzle a little extra hot sauce over the top only if you are making the medium version.
  2. Bake 15-18 minutes, until hot at the edges and thick enough to scoop.
  3. If the top looks pale, broil for 1-2 minutes, watching closely.
  4. Top with sliced green onions. Add a squeeze of lemon only if it needs brightness at the end.
Pita Chips
Use the pitas you already bought

Make scoops
  1. Cut pita into sturdy wedges. Brush or toss lightly with olive oil.
  2. Season with a little salt, plus garlic powder or smoked paprika if you want.
  3. Bake at 400 F for 6-10 minutes, flipping once, until crisp at the edges.
  4. Let them cool for a few minutes so they firm up before scooping the dip.
