Buffalo Chickpea Dip
Serves 4 · ~25 minutes
Pull Out
Ingredients
Chickpeas Plain hummus Hot sauce Lemon Green onions Garlic powder Smoked paprika Cayenne Olive oil Small baking dish Chips
Make Dip
Mild base, optional heat

Build the base
  1. Heat oven to 375 F. Drain and rinse 1 can chickpeas.
  2. Mash about two-thirds of the chickpeas in a bowl. Leave the rest mostly whole so the dip has some texture.
  3. Stir in 1/2 cup plain hummus, 2 tbsp buffalo-style hot sauce, 2 tsp olive oil, juice from 1/2 lemon, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, and 1/4 tsp smoked paprika.
  4. For a medium batch: add another 2 tbsp buffalo-style hot sauce and 1/8 tsp cayenne. Skip this step for a mild batch.
Bake
Warm and scoopable

Finish it
  1. Spread into a small baking dish. Drizzle a little extra hot sauce over the top only if you are making the medium version.
  2. Bake 15-18 minutes, until hot at the edges and thick enough to scoop.
  3. Top with sliced green onions and a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Pack with sturdy tortilla chips, pita chips, or celery sticks.
