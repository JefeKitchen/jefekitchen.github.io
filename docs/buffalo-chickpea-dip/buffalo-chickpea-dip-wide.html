Buffalo Chickpea Dip
Serves 4
Prep ~10 min
Cook ~20 min
Mix

Build the base

ChickpeasHummusHot sauceFresh garlicSmoked paprikaCayenne
  1. Heat oven to 400 F. Drain and rinse 1 can chickpeas.
  2. Mash about two-thirds of the chickpeas in a bowl. Keep the last third mostly whole.
  3. Stir in the whole chickpeas, 1/2 cup hummus, 2 tbsp hot sauce, 2 tsp olive oil, 1 small grated garlic clove, and smoked paprika.
  4. For a medium batch: add another 2 tbsp hot sauce and 1/8 tsp cayenne. Skip this for mild.
Bake

Warm it through

DipGreen onions
  1. Spread into a small baking dish. Drizzle extra hot sauce on top only for the medium version.
  2. Bake 15-18 minutes, until hot at the edges and scoopable.
  3. If the top looks pale, broil for 1-2 minutes, watching closely.
  4. Top with green onions. Add lemon only if it needs brightness.
Pita Chips

Make scoops

PitaOlive oilSalt
  1. Cut pita into sturdy wedges and brush or toss lightly with olive oil.
  2. Season with salt, plus garlic powder or smoked paprika if you want.
  3. Bake at 400 F for 6-10 minutes, flipping once, until crisp at the edges.
  4. Let them cool for a few minutes so they firm up before scooping the dip.
