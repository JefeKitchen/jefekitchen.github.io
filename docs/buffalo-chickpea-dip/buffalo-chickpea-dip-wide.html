Buffalo Chickpea Dip
Serves 4
~25 minutes
Mix

Build the base

ChickpeasHummusHot sauceLemonFresh garlicSmoked paprikaCayenne
  1. Heat oven to 400 F. Drain and rinse 1 can chickpeas.
  2. Mash about two-thirds of the chickpeas in a bowl, leaving some texture.
  3. Stir in 1/2 cup hummus, 2 tbsp hot sauce, 2 tsp olive oil, 1 small grated garlic clove, and smoked paprika.
  4. For a medium batch: add another 2 tbsp hot sauce and 1/8 tsp cayenne. Skip this for mild.
Bake

Warm it through

DipGreen onionsChips
  1. Spread into a small baking dish. Drizzle extra hot sauce on top only for the medium version.
  2. Bake 15-18 minutes, until hot at the edges and scoopable.
  3. If the top looks pale, broil for 1-2 minutes, watching closely.
  4. Top with green onions. Add lemon only if it needs brightness.
Pack

Take it snack-ready

  1. Let it cool slightly if transporting.
  2. Pack with sturdy tortilla chips, pita chips, or celery sticks.
  3. If reheating later, warm gently until scoopable again.
