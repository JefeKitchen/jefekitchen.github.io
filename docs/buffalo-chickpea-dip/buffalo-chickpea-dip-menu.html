Dip
Buffalo Chickpea Dip
Mild base · optional medium heat
Mashed chickpeas baked with plain hummus, buffalo-style hot sauce, fresh garlic, and smoked paprika. Add extra hot sauce and a little cayenne only when you want the medium version; finish with lemon only if it needs brightness.
