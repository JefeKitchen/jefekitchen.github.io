MACARONI
& FROMAGE
Hand Crafted · Aged Cheese Sauce · Totally Not a Box of Annie's
📦

A Note on SourcingThe ingredients for this dish arrive pre-measured in a proprietary format. This is called efficiency. Do not read the box in front of anyone.

Prep
Sarah · Mise en Place

Gather the Components

🧀
  1. Locate one box of pasta. Open it. Inside you will find a bag of pasta and a packet of cheese powder. Both are needed. Do not discard the packet. The packet is the whole point.
  2. Set aside 2 tbsp butter and ¼ cup milk. These are the supporting cast. They matter more than they appear to.
  3. Locate a pot large enough for the pasta to move freely. Pasta needs space. Respect this.
Cook
Sarah · The Long Game

Boil the Water

💧
  1. Fill the pot with water and place on the stove over high heat. Add a generous pinch of salt. Salted water is the foundation of all great pasta. This is what separates the serious cook from the casual one.
  2. Wait for a full rolling boil. Not a simmer. Not some bubbles. A full boil. This will take longer than expected. This is not your fault. Water is slow. It always has been.
Bella · Investigating

Water Quality Check

🐾
  1. Bella is here. She is sniffing the pot. She approves of the water. Her input was not requested but has been noted. She may stay.
Sarah · The Pasta

Cook the Macaroni

🍝
  1. Add the pasta to the boiling water. Stir immediately so it doesn't stick together. This is the most active this recipe gets. Enjoy it.
  2. Cook for 8–9 minutes, stirring occasionally. The pasta is done when it is tender but still has a little bite. This is called al dente. Tell people that.
  3. Reserve a splash of pasta water before draining — about 2 tablespoons. Set it aside. It may be needed. It may not be. Having it is the professional move.
  4. Drain the pasta. Do not rinse it. Rinsing removes the starch. The starch is your friend. Keep your friends.
Finish
Sarah · The Sauce

Build the Fromage

✨
  1. Return the drained pasta to the pot over low heat.
  2. Add 2 tbsp butter and stir until melted and coating the pasta. This is the moment everything comes together. Acknowledge it.
  3. Add ¼ cup milk. Stir.
  4. Open the cheese powder packet. Pour it in. All of it. Do not second-guess the packet. The packet knows what it is doing. Stir until smooth and glossy.
  5. If the sauce seems too thick, add a small splash of the reserved pasta water. If it seems too thin, cook for another minute, stirring constantly. You are a chef now. Adjust accordingly.
Sarah · Plating

The Presentation

🍽️
  1. Spoon into bowls. Do not serve from the pot. We are not animals.
  2. Accept all compliments. This dish took effort. The effort was real even if the cheese was powder. Do not let anyone tell you otherwise.
