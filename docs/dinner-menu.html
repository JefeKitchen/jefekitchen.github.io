The Mains
✦
Dijon Butter Chicken
Chicken thighs seared in butter until golden, finished with a rich pan sauce of Dijon mustard and a splash of cream or broth. Simple, French-leaning, deeply savory.
Honey Mustard Baked Chicken
Chicken thighs or breasts glazed in a mix of honey, yellow mustard, Dijon, and a little butter, then baked until sticky and golden. Sweet, tangy, and effortless.
French Onion Chicken
Seared chicken thighs topped with slow-caramelized white onion, melted Gruyère, and a Dijon pan sauce. Rich, deeply savory, and a little bit special.
Sides
✦
Caramelized Onions Slow-cooked white onion in butter until golden and jammy. Goes with all three dishes.
Dijon Roasted Potatoes Potatoes tossed in Dijon, butter, salt and pepper, roasted at 425°F until crispy.
Simple Green Salad Greens with a quick Dijon vinaigrette — Dijon, olive oil, a splash of vinegar, salt and pepper.
Buttered Rice Simple white rice finished with butter and salt. A good base for any of the three sauces.
Crusty Bread For mopping up the pan sauce. Essential with the Dijon Butter or French Onion.
