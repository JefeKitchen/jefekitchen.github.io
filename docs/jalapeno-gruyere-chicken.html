JALAPEÑO
GRUYÈRE
CHICKEN
With Bok Choy · Serves 2–4 · Do Not Be Cyril
Ingredients · Scaled for 2–4
  • Boneless skin-on chicken thighs 4
  • Jalapeño, thinly sliced 2
  • Gruyère, grated 90g
  • Bok choy, quartered lengthwise 1 regular
  • Garlic cloves, minced 4
  • Heavy cream 6 tbsp
  • Chicken broth 4 tbsp
  • Olive oil 2 tbsp
  • Butter 1 tbsp
  • Smoked paprika ½ tsp
  • Salt & pepper aggressively
Prep
Step 1 · Critical Foundation

Season the Chicken

🧂
  1. Pat the chicken thighs completely dry with paper towels. Moisture is the enemy of a good sear, and the enemy of a good sear is the enemy of me.
  2. Season aggressively with salt, pepper, and smoked paprika. Yes, that much. More. There you go.
Theo · Unsolicited

Pre-Cook Inspection

🐾
  1. Theo is here. He has assessed the chicken and has opinions. He cannot have raw chicken, jalapeño, garlic, or Gruyère. He will remain present anyway. This is his process.
Cook
Step 2 · Do Not Blow This

Sear the Chicken

🍳
  1. Heat olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high until it shimmers. Lay the chicken in skin-side down. Do not touch it. Do not poke it. Do not talk to it. Leave it alone for 7–8 minutes until the skin is deeply golden.
  2. Flip. Cook 3 more minutes. Remove to a plate. It is not fully cooked yet. This is intentional.
Step 3 · Krieger Supervises

The Jalapeño Cream Sauce

🌶️
  1. Drop heat to medium. Add butter to the skillet. Add jalapeño and garlic. Sauté 2 minutes.
  2. Deglaze with chicken broth and scrape up all the browned bits from the bottom. That's flavor. Don't leave it behind.
  3. Pour in heavy cream. Simmer 2–3 minutes until slightly thickened and coats a spoon. If Theo tries to get near the sauce, give him a look.
Step 4 · Chain of Command

Deploy the Bok Choy

🥬
  1. Nestle the bok choy cut-side down into the sauce around the edges. Little green operatives on a perimeter. Regular bok choy is quartered so it should fit — if pieces are large, cut down further so they cook through.
  2. Return the chicken skin-side up to the center of the pan. Spoon sauce over the top. The chicken is in command.
Finish
Step 5 · Eyes On · Broiler Op

Gruyère Deployment

🧀
  1. Shower Gruyère over the chicken thighs.
  2. Move the skillet to the top rack under the broiler. Broil 3–4 minutes until the cheese is molten and golden. Watch it the entire time. Gruyère burns under a broiler faster than you think. Do not be Cyril.
Step 6 · Mission Complete

Rest & Plate

🍽️
  1. Rest 2 minutes. Plate directly from the skillet. Spoon the jalapeño cream sauce over everything with the confidence of someone who just completed a flawless extraction.
  2. Serve with rice or crusty bread. You've earned a drink. KA-KAAAW.
Theo · Closing Statement

Post-Cook Assessment

🐾
  1. Theo has supervised this entire operation and has received nothing. The jalapeño, garlic, Gruyère, and cream are all off limits. He is aware. He would like to file a complaint. There is no complaints department. Goodnight, Theo.

On the Jalapeño

Seeds in for real heat. Seeds out is something Cyril would do. Don't be Cyril.

On the Gruyère

It will burn faster than you expect under the broiler. Eyes on the skillet at all times. This is non-negotiable.

On the Bok Choy

Using regular full-size bok choy — quarter it lengthwise so it cooks through. Baby bok choy just needs halving but regular is too thick and won't cook through if only halved.

⏱️

Total Time

About 25–30 minutes. One skillet, one cutting board. The broiler is the only part that requires your full attention. Give it your full attention.
