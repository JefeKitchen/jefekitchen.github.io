Charcoal Chicken
Serves 2–3 · Prep ~15 min · Cook ~45 min
Prep First
Before lighting

Season the chicken
  1. Pat 2 lb chicken thighs dry. Season with 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt, black pepper, 1 tsp smoked paprika, grated garlic, lemon zest, and a thin coat of olive oil.
  2. Let the chicken sit at room temp while you set up the grill. This takes the chill off and gives the salt time to work.
  3. Shuck the corn, halve the lemon, chop parsley, and measure sauce ingredients before the fire is ready.
Charcoal Setup
Gas-grill brain translation

Build two heat zones
  1. Open the bottom vent and lid vent fully. Airflow is your temperature knob on charcoal.
  2. Fill a chimney starter about 2/3 full with charcoal. Light it over a starter cube or newspaper. Do not add lighter fluid.
  3. Wait until the top coals are mostly gray at the edges with a little flame still visible, usually 15-20 minutes.
  4. Pour the hot coals onto one side of the grill. Leave the other side empty. That gives you a hot direct side and a cooler indirect side.
  5. Put the grate on, close the lid for 5 minutes, then scrape and oil the grate. Position the open lid vent over the chicken/cool side so heat pulls across the meat like a little convection oven.
Cook
Direct then indirect

Grill the chicken
  1. Start chicken over the coals. Sear 3-4 minutes per side with the lid open, just long enough to build color.
  2. Move chicken to the cooler side. Close the lid and cook indirectly until the thickest piece reaches 175 F. Thighs are safe at 165 F, but 175 F is juicier and more tender.
  3. If the grill is racing hot, close the bottom vent halfway. If it starts fading, open vents fully and crack the lid for a minute.
  4. Rest chicken 5-10 minutes before slicing. Resting matters more with charcoal because the outside gets intense heat.
Sauce
Use the broth

Horseradish-Dijon broth sauce
  1. Simmer 1 cup chicken broth with 1 tbsp Dijon, 1 tsp honey, and a squeeze of lemon until slightly reduced.
  2. Take off heat. Whisk in 2 tbsp sour cream and 1-2 tbsp horseradish. Do not boil after dairy goes in.
  3. Spoon over sliced chicken and finish with parsley.
Sides
While the chicken rests

Grilled corn salad
  1. Grill corn over direct heat, turning until charred in spots, about 8-10 minutes.
  2. Cut kernels off the cob. Toss with butter or olive oil, lemon juice, parsley, salt, pepper, and a tiny spoon of horseradish if you want a little bite.
  3. Serve with cucumber slices or a simple green salad if you want something cold next to the smoky chicken.
