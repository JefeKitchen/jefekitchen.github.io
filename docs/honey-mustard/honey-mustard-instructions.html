Prep
Jeff · First Thing

Preheat the Oven

🌡️
  1. Turn oven to 400°F. Do this before anything else so it's fully up to temp by the time the potatoes are ready.
Jaya · While Jeff Preps Potatoes

Make the Glaze

🍯
  1. In a small bowl whisk together 3 tbsp Dijon, 3 tbsp yellow mustard, 4 tbsp honey, and 2 tbsp melted butter. Season with salt and pepper.
  2. Taste it. Should be sweet, tangy, and a little sharp. Adjust honey or mustard to your liking.
  3. Split into two separate bowls — one for cooking, one for the table as a dipping sauce. Once a bowl touches raw chicken it never goes on the table. Keep them separate from here.
Theo · Quality Control

Honey Taste Test

🐾
  1. Let Theo lick a tiny bit of honey off your finger. He has been present in the kitchen for 30 seconds and already considers himself the head chef. Besides, you were going to do it anyway.
Jeff · Potatoes First

Prep the Potatoes

🥔
  1. Wash the potatoes. Cut into roughly 1-inch chunks — no need to peel them.
  2. In a large bowl toss with 2 tbsp Dijon, 2 tbsp olive oil, a generous pinch of salt, and black pepper.
  3. Spread in a single layer on a sheet pan. Don't crowd them or they'll steam instead of crisp.
Jaya · While Jeff Preps Chicken

Make the Vinaigrette

🥗
  1. In a small jar or bowl combine 1 tsp Dijon, 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar, salt and pepper.
  2. Whisk or shake until emulsified. Taste and adjust — more vinegar for tang, more oil to mellow it out.
  3. Set aside. Don't dress the greens until right before serving or they'll wilt.
Jeff · Chicken

Prep the Chicken

🍗
  1. Pat chicken thighs dry with paper towels — key for getting a good crust without skin.
  2. Season both sides generously with salt and pepper.
  3. Brush a light layer of glaze from the cooking bowl over the top of each thigh. Just a thin coat — the heavy glaze goes on halfway through. This gets flavor in early without burning.
  4. Place on a baking dish or sheet pan.
Cook
Jeff · Oven · 400°F

Roast Everything

🔥
  1. Put the potatoes in the oven first. Set a timer for 10 minutes.
  2. After 10 minutes, add the chicken to the oven on a separate rack.
  3. At the halfway point for the chicken (~15 min in), flip the potatoes and brush a generous layer of glaze from the cooking bowl over the chicken. This is when the caramelization happens — don't skip it.
  4. Chicken is done when internal temp hits 165°F — boneless skinless thighs will be ready in roughly 20–25 min. Check early and don't let them go over or they'll dry out.
  5. Potatoes are done when golden and crispy on the edges — roughly 40–45 min total. Soft inside, crispy outside.
Theo · Well Earned

Final Taste Test

🐾
  1. While the chicken rests, pull off a small piece of plain cooked chicken — no glaze, no seasoning — and give it to Theo. He has supervised this entire cook and his feedback has been invaluable.
  2. Also give him a small plain cooked potato chunk. He has been staring at them for 40 minutes. It's the right thing to do.
Jaya · While Chicken Rests

Finish the Salad

🥗
  1. Once the chicken comes out of the oven, dress the greens with the vinaigrette. Toss well and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Serve immediately alongside the chicken and potatoes.
⏱️

Total Time

~45 minutes start to finish at 400°F. Potatoes go in first, chicken 10 minutes later. Boneless skinless thighs cook fast — check temp at 20 minutes. Heavy glaze goes on the chicken at the halfway mark. Two glaze bowls — cooking and table. Never mix them.
