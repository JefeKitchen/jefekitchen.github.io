Honey Mustard
Serves 2-3
~45 minutes
Potatoes

Start the side

PotatoesOlive oilSaltPepper
  1. Heat oven to 425 F. Cut potatoes into small even pieces.
  2. Toss with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread cut-side down with room between pieces.
  3. Roast while you prep chicken so the potatoes get a head start.
Sauce

Mix honey mustard

HoneyDijonGarlicButterLemon
  1. Stir honey, Dijon, garlic, melted butter, and a squeeze of lemon.
  2. Taste it. Add more Dijon if it needs bite or honey if it needs roundness.
  3. Keep some sauce clean for finishing if you want extra at the table.
Roast

Chicken and finish

  1. Season chicken with salt and pepper. Nestle it near the potatoes once they have roasted about 15 minutes.
  2. Brush chicken with sauce and roast until browned and cooked through to 165 F.
  3. If potatoes need more color, pull chicken to rest and give potatoes a few more minutes.
  4. Serve with the extra honey mustard spooned over the top.
