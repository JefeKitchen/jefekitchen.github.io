Prep
  1. 1
    Preheat the Oven
    Turn oven to 400°F. Do this before anything else.
  2. 2
    Make the Glaze
    Whisk together 3 tbsp Dijon, 3 tbsp yellow mustard, 4 tbsp honey, and 2 tbsp melted butter. Season with salt and pepper. Split into two separate bowls — one for cooking, one for the table. Once the cooking bowl touches raw chicken it never goes on the table.
  3. 3
    Theo's Honey Tax
    Let Theo lick a tiny bit of honey off your finger. He has been present in the kitchen for 30 seconds and already considers himself the head chef. Besides, you were going to do it anyway.
  4. 4
    Prep the Potatoes
    Cut potatoes into 1-inch chunks — no need to peel. Toss in a bowl with 2 tbsp Dijon, 2 tbsp avocado or olive oil, a generous pinch of salt, and black pepper. Spread in a single layer on a sheet pan. Don't crowd them.
  5. 5
    Make the Vinaigrette
    In a small jar or bowl combine 1 tsp Dijon, 3 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar, salt and pepper. Whisk or shake until combined. Set aside — don't dress the greens until right before serving.
  6. 6
    Prep the Chicken
    Pat chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Season both sides with salt and pepper. Brush a light coat of glaze from the cooking bowl over the top. Just a thin layer — the heavy glaze goes on halfway through. Place on a separate baking dish or pan.
Oven
  1. 7
    ⏱ Set Timer · 10 Min
    Potatoes In
    Put the potatoes in the oven. Start your timer.
  2. 8
    ⏱ Timer Goes Off · Set Another · 10–12 Min
    Chicken In
    Put the chicken in the oven on a separate rack. Reset your timer.
  3. 9
    ⏱ Timer Goes Off · Set Another · 10 Min
    Flip Potatoes · Heavy Glaze on Chicken
    Flip the potatoes. Brush a generous coat of glaze from the cooking bowl over the chicken — top and sides. This is where the caramelization happens. Reset your timer.
  4. 10
    ⏱ Timer Goes Off
    Check the Chicken
    Check the internal temp. Boneless skinless thighs move fast. Pull at 165°F and rest on the counter. Don't let them go over. Potatoes still have more time — leave them in.
Finish
  1. 11
    Dress the Salad
    While the chicken rests, toss the salad greens with the vinaigrette. Season with salt and pepper. Don't do this too early or the greens will wilt.
  2. 12
    ~40–45 Min Total In Oven
    Potatoes Out
    Potatoes are done when golden and crispy on the edges — soft inside, crispy outside. Pull and serve alongside the chicken and salad.
  3. 13
    Table Glaze
    Put the second bowl of glaze on the table as a dipping sauce. This is the clean bowl that never touched raw chicken.
🌡️

Oven · 400°F

Chicken pulls at 165°F internal. Check early — boneless skinless goes fast.

Glaze

  • Dijon mustard 3 tbsp
  • Yellow mustard 3 tbsp
  • Honey 4 tbsp
  • Butter, melted 2 tbsp
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Potatoes

  • Dijon mustard 2 tbsp
  • Avocado or olive oil 2 tbsp
  • Salt & pepper generous

Vinaigrette

  • Dijon mustard 1 tsp
  • Oil 3 tbsp
  • Apple cider vinegar 1 tbsp
  • Salt & pepper to taste
