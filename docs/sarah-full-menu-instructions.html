Sarah · The Pasta

Macaroni & Fromage

🧀
  1. Add the pasta and cook 8–9 minutes, stirring occasionally. Reserve a splash of pasta water before draining. Drain. Do not rinse. The starch is your friend.
  2. Return to the pot on low heat. Add 2 tbsp butter and stir until melted. Add ¼ cup milk. Stir.
  3. Open the cheese powder packet. Pour it in. All of it. Do not second-guess the packet. The packet knows. Stir until smooth. If too thick, add a splash of pasta water.
  4. Spoon into bowls. Not the pot. We are not animals. Accept all compliments.
