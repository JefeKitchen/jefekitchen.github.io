Chicken Skewers
Serves 2–3 · ~55 minutes
Drink
Pull Out
Gin Soda water Cucumber Lemon Honey Ice
Cold and bright

Cucumber gin highball
  1. If using honey, loosen it first: stir 1 part honey with 1 part warm water to make a quick honey syrup. Use 1 tsp honey syrup per drink, or swap in simple syrup.
  2. In a glass, stir 2 oz gin, 1/2 oz lemon juice, and the honey syrup until combined.
  3. Add ice and a few thin cucumber slices. Top with soda water.
  4. Give it one gentle stir. Taste and add a little more lemon or syrup if you want it sharper or softer.
Prep First
Pull Out
Chicken thighs Skewers Pita Lemons Garlic Olive oil Paprika Oregano Dijon Salt & pepper
Before lighting

Marinate and organize
  1. If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for 30 minutes so they do not scorch immediately.
  2. Cut chicken thighs into 1 1/2 inch pieces. Toss with olive oil, lemon zest, lemon juice, grated garlic, 1 tsp kosher salt, pepper, smoked paprika, oregano, and 1 tsp Dijon.
  3. Thread chicken onto skewers with a tiny bit of space between pieces. Crowded skewers steam instead of grill.
Light Charcoal
Start before the sides

Get the chimney going
  1. Open the bottom vent and lid vent all the way. Charcoal heat is controlled by air: more open means hotter, more closed means cooler.
  2. Set the chimney starter on the charcoal grate, not on a table, deck, or plastic surface.
  3. Put 1 lighter cube under the chimney. Fill the chimney about 2/3 full for this cook. Skip lighter fluid.
  4. Light the cube through a lower chimney hole. Leave it alone for 15-20 minutes while the fire climbs upward.
  5. The coals are ready when the top pieces are gray around the edges and you can see orange glow down in the chimney. If the top is still mostly black, wait.
Sauce
Pull Out
Chicken broth Greek yogurt Horseradish Honey Dijon Lemon Pepper
Make while coals light

Creamy dipping sauce
  1. Simmer 1 cup chicken broth with 1 tbsp Dijon, 1 tsp honey, and a squeeze of lemon until reduced by about a third.
  2. Cool for a minute. Whisk in 1/3 cup Greek yogurt or sour cream and 1-2 tbsp horseradish. Season with pepper and more lemon.
  3. Keep warm but do not boil after the dairy goes in. Serve as a dip for chicken and pita.
Cold Side
Pull Out
Cucumber Tomatoes Parsley Lemon Olive oil Salt & pepper
Also while coals light

Cucumber tomato salad
  1. Slice cucumber, halve tomatoes, and thinly slice a little red onion if using.
  2. Toss with olive oil, lemon juice, parsley, salt, and pepper.
  3. Set aside until the skewers and pita are done.
Build Fire
Two-zone setup

Hot side and safe side
  1. Carefully pour the lit coals onto one side of the grill. Leave the other side mostly empty.
  2. Put the cooking grate on, close the lid, and let the grill run hot for 10-15 minutes to burn off old buildup.
  3. Scrape the hot grate hard with a grill brush or a ball of foil held in tongs.
  4. Oil the grate lightly with a folded paper towel held in tongs. Do not soak it so much that oil drips into the coals.
  5. Put the lid vent over the cooler side so heat and smoke travel across the food instead of straight out.
Grill
Pull Out
Skewers Pita
Skewers cook fast

Chicken and pita
  1. Start skewers over direct heat. Grill with the lid open, turning every 2-3 minutes, until the outside has good color.
  2. If the chicken is browning too fast or dripping causes flare-ups, slide skewers to the cooler side and close the lid.
  3. Cook chicken to 165 F. Thigh pieces usually take 10-14 minutes total depending on heat.
  4. Warm pita for 30-60 seconds per side at the end. Watch closely; bread burns fast over charcoal.
Dessert
Pull Out
Extra pita Greek yogurt Honey Cinnamon Vanilla Salt
Use the last heat

Honey-cinnamon pita chips
  1. Cut extra pita or naan into wedges. Brush lightly with olive oil and sprinkle with cinnamon and a tiny pinch of salt.
  2. Grill over the cooler side for 30-60 seconds per side, just until warm, crisp at the edges, and lightly marked. Watch closely; they go fast.
  3. Stir Greek yogurt with honey, a splash of vanilla, and a pinch of cinnamon.
  4. Serve the warm pita chips with the honey yogurt dip.
Shut Down
After eating

Close the vents
  1. Close the bottom vent and lid vent all the way to starve the coals of oxygen.
  2. Leave ash and spent coals in the grill until fully cool. Empty them later into a metal ash bucket or lidded metal container.
