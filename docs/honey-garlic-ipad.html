HONEY GARLIC CHICKEN
Serves2–3
Time~25 Min
Dishes1 Skillet
Ingredients
Cucumber½
Chicken thighs4–6
Garlic cloves6
Broccoli2 handfuls
Honey3 tbsp
Soy sauce3 tbsp
Chicken broth½ cup
Cornstarch1 tbsp
Butter1 tbsp
Olive oil1 tbsp
Jeff
Prep
  1. 1
    Pat chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
Cook — Sear
  1. 1
    Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high. Sear chicken smooth-side down 5–6 minutes without touching until golden. Flip and cook 4 minutes more. Remove to a plate.
Cook — Sauce
  1. 1
    Drop heat to medium. Add butter. Add minced garlic and cook 1–2 minutes, stirring constantly, until fragrant and just golden. Do not let it burn.
  2. 2
    Pour in the honey soy broth mixture. Bring to a simmer, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom.
  3. 3
    Stir in the cornstarch slurry. Keep stirring — it thickens fast. Add a splash of water if it gets too thick.
  4. 4
    Return chicken to the pan with the broccoli florets. Spoon sauce over everything. Simmer on low 5–6 minutes until chicken is cooked through and broccoli is tender.
Jaya
Make Ahead
  1. 1
    Slice cucumber into rounds about ¼ inch thick. Arrange on a plate.
  2. 2
    Drizzle with honey and a small splash of soy sauce. Sesame seeds on top if you have them. Cover and refrigerate.
Prep
  1. 1
    Mince 6 garlic cloves. This is a lot of garlic. That's correct. That's the dish.
  2. 2
    Cut broccoli into small florets. Set aside.
  3. 3
    Whisk together 3 tbsp honey, 3 tbsp soy sauce, and ½ cup chicken broth in a small bowl. Set aside.
  4. 4
    In a separate small bowl mix 1 tbsp cornstarch with 2 tbsp cold water until smooth. Set aside.
Cook — Rice
  1. 1
    Rinse rice under cold water until mostly clear. Cook per package instructions — bring to boil, reduce to low, cover, 18 minutes. Do not lift the lid.
Plate & Serve
  1. 1
    Fluff the rice with a fork. Spoon into bowls or onto plates.
  2. 2
    Lay the chicken on top. Spoon sauce and broccoli florets generously over everything. Garnish with fresh parsley if using.
  3. 3
    Put the cucumber bites out on the table. Dinner is ready.
